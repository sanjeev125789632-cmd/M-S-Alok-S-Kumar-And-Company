How our engagements run

  1. Enquiry and scoping call. You tell us the position; we ask for the registrations, past filings and books that are relevant. Usually 15–20 minutes on the phone or in the Rohini office.
  2. Written scope and fee. Our fees are transparent and quoted upfront based on the scope of work, with no hidden charges. You approve before work starts.
  3. Document collection. A checklist goes to you with exactly what is needed. Documents can be dropped at the office or shared through secure digital exchange.
  4. Preparation and reconciliation. Returns are matched against source data — GSTR-2B for input credit, Form 26AS and AIS for income tax — and discrepancies are resolved before filing.
  5. Filing, acknowledgement and record. We file, share the acknowledgement, and keep a working file so the next period, notice or assessment starts from complete records.
  6. Ongoing calendar management. For retainer clients, upcoming due dates are tracked and you are reminded ahead of time rather than after the deadline.
