Frequently asked

Questions Delhi clients ask us most

What documents are required for GST registration?

GST registration generally requires PAN and Aadhaar details, a photograph, bank information and evidence of the principal place of business. Companies and LLPs also provide constitution documents and authorised-signatory evidence. The exact list depends on the applicant type and premises arrangement; review the GST registration document checklist before submitting.

How often do I need to file GST returns?

Return frequency depends on the registration and filing scheme. Regular taxpayers generally file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly, while eligible taxpayers under QRMP file those returns quarterly and make monthly payments. Other statements or annual filings may apply separately. Confirm the current cycle on the GST Portal or with the professional handling the registration.

Do you serve clients outside Sector 9, Rohini?

Yes. M/S Alok S Kumar And Company meets clients at its Sector 9, Rohini office and can review documents through online exchange for clients elsewhere in Delhi NCR and India. Whether the entire engagement can be completed remotely depends on the filing, identity verification and any authority-specific requirement.

What is your fee structure?

The firm reviews the matter before confirming the scope and professional fee. Government filing fees, stamp duty and other third-party costs are identified separately when they apply. Because the work required depends on the records, filing history and deadlines, the website does not publish a single fee for every case.

How can I book a consultation?

A consultation normally begins with a short description of the filing, registration, notice or accounting issue and the relevant period. You can call, use WhatsApp or send an enquiry. The firm then identifies the initial documents needed and confirms the scope before professional work starts.
