## 0. Three premise corrections

### 0.1 "CA Alok Kumar" is not this firm's founder

This firm's Chartered Accountant is **Alok S Kumar**, B.Com (Hons), FCA, ICAI M.No. 094095
(`about/index.html`, "Alok S Kumar ... is the Chartered Accountant associated with M/S Alok S
Kumar And Company"). The firm's own copy never says "founder" — it says "associated with".

"CA Alok Kumar" is a **different, established Delhi practitioner** — at least two of them:

| Entity | Evidence |
|---|---|
| CA Alok Kumar, FCA — Dwarka + Rajendra Place, Senior Partner, S.K. Mehta & Co. | owns exact-match domain `caalokkumar.com`, plus `caindwarka.com` |
| CA Alok Kumar — K Alok & Associates, New Delhi | `kalok.in` |
| "CA Alok Kumar" LinkedIn | `linkedin.com/in/ca-alok-kumar-188837185/` |

**Consequence.** `ca alok kumar` at position 6.04 is not a brand query being lost. It is
partial-match spillover onto another practitioner's personal brand, against an exact-match
domain. 1 click / 27 impressions (3.7%) is what spillover looks like. Targeting that string
would (a) lose, and (b) assert that this firm's CA is a person he is not.

The entity string shipped is therefore **`CA Alok S Kumar`**.

Related: `ca in dwarka` (2 impressions, pos 21) is almost certainly the same contamination —
Dwarka is the other CA Alok Kumar's office location, not this firm's.

## 4. Structured data

### 4.1 Why the Search Appearance report is empty

Per §0.2, the markup exists and parses. The report is empty because **of the schema types on
this site, only one can ever produce a Search Appearance row.**

| Type | Present? | Rich result in India, Sept 2026 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BreadcrumbList` | yes, all 15 pages | **Yes** — produces a Breadcrumbs row in Search Appearance | The only realistic row this site can earn |
| `FAQPage` | yes, 7 pages, 5–6 Q each | **No.** Google restricted FAQ rich results in Aug 2023 to well-known authoritative government and health sites. A CA firm will not qualify. | Entity understanding only. Keep — do not expand |
| `BlogPosting` / Article | yes, 3 posts | **No visible enhancement** for a non-news publisher. Feeds Top Stories / Discover eligibility and author-entity understanding. | Entity only |
| `AccountingService` / `LocalBusiness` | yes, sitewide | **Not a Search Appearance item at all.** Feeds the knowledge panel and local pack. | Entity only |
| `Organization`, `Person`, `WebSite`, `WebPage` | yes | No | Entity only |
| `Service` | yes, 7 pages | No rich result exists for `Service` | Entity only |
| `AggregateRating` / `Review` | **no** | Self-serving reviews on your own domain are ignored by Google (2019 policy), **and** prohibited as testimonials under ICAI | **Do not add.** Two independent reasons |
| `Sitelinks Searchbox` | no | Deprecated by Google, 2024 | Do not add |
| `HowTo` | no | Deprecated by Google, 2023 | Do not add |

So "empty Search Appearance" is roughly 85% expected behaviour, not a defect. The residual
question is only why **Breadcrumbs** has no row.

## 5. Location pages

### 5.1 URL pattern

```
/ca-in-rohini/          <- SHIPPED
/ca-in-chandni-chowk/   <- SHIPPED (practice confirmed it serves Chandni Chowk clients)
/ca-in-dwarka/          <- do not build
```

**Why this pattern.** It mirrors the query syntax exactly (`ca in rohini`, `ca in dwarka` are
live queries in the data). It is flat — one click from root, no depth dilution. And critically
it is **not** a service×location matrix (`/gst-registration-rohini/`,
`/gst-registration-dwarka/`, …). Seven services × four localities = 28 near-identical pages,
which is the exact shape Google's doorway-page policy was written for. One page per locality,
each covering all services *through that locality's lens*, stays defensible.

### 5.2 Why not Dwarka

Two independent reasons:
1. `ca in dwarka` is 2 impressions at pos 21 — and Dwarka is the office location of the *other* CA Alok Kumar (§0.1), who owns `caindwarka.com`. That query is contamination, not demand.
2. The firm has no Dwarka presence. A page claiming to serve Dwarka, with no office, no local facts and no staff there, is thin duplication of the Rohini page with the nouns swapped. That is the doorway pattern.

Chandni Chowk is different — 7 impressions at pos 38 is the **second-highest query** in the
set, and Chandni Chowk is a dense trader/wholesale market with genuinely distinct compliance
needs. **The practice confirmed it serves Chandni Chowk clients, so the page is built.**

Note the asymmetry that makes it defensible: it is a *service-area* page, not a location page.
It states in the body that there is one office and it is in Rohini, and it earns its place on
subject matter — wholesale trade compliance — rather than on a claimed local presence.

### 5.4 Doorway-page defence — concrete thresholds

**Measured result for the shipped pair** (locality nouns normalised to a placeholder, body text
only, TO FILL comments excluded):

```
Rohini body words        : 904
Chandni Chowk body words : 1557
Shared word runs         : 90
Overlap vs smaller page  : 10.0%   (rule 1 ceiling: 60%)  -> PASS
Locality-unique facts, Rohini        : Rithala, Red Line, s.44ADA, Sector 5
Locality-unique facts, Chandni Chowk : Khari Baoli, s.269ST, Bhagirath Palace, composition
```

Re-run this before adding any third locality page. The test script is the one in the commit for
`4f24152`; it normalises `rohini|chandni chowk|old delhi|sector 9|north-west delhi` and diffs
the `<main>` bodies.

A locality page in this set is **thin duplication** if any of these is true:

1. **>60% of its body text is shared with another locality page after removing the locality noun.** Test: diff `/ca-in-rohini/` against `/ca-in-chandni-chowk/` with `rohini|chandni chowk` normalised to a placeholder. If the diff is under 40% of the body, the second page should not ship.
2. **It contains no fact that is false for the other locality.** The GST ward, IT circle and Sub-Registrar office are the load-bearing facts here — they genuinely differ by locality, they are verifiable, and they are useful. If a page has none of them, it is a template fill.
3. **It claims a presence that does not exist.** No "our Dwarka office", no "visit us in Chandni Chowk", no fabricated local landmark. One office, stated plainly, on every page.
4. **It duplicates the service pages rather than routing to them.** Each service section on the locality page must be ≤3 sentences and must link out. If a locality page starts explaining GSTR-3B in depth, it is competing with `/services/gst/` and cannibalising it.
5. **It exists only for a query, not for a reader.** The test: would a Rohini business owner who already knows the firm's name find the page useful? If the only value is keyword match, it is a doorway.

Rule of thumb for this site: **one locality page per office or per genuinely-served commercial
district, capped at two total.** Three or more localities for a single-office firm with 341
monthly impressions is not a content strategy, it is a footprint.

### 6.1 GST — `301` the blog post into the service page

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **SHIPPED** — commit `534d600` |
| **Canonical target** | `/services/gst/` |
| **Action on the other URL** | `301` `/blog/gst-return-filing-guide-delhi/` → `/services/gst/`, after merging its content |
| **Not chosen** | keep-and-relink, canonical-only |

**Justification.** The queries are intent-ambiguous, not split: `gst return services in delhi`
(pos 40), `gst return filing in delhi` (pos 78), `gst registration consultant in delhi`
(pos 61.2) — all commercial-investigational, all currently unserved because Google is choosing
neither page. Three further facts decide it:

- The site has **15 pages and 9 clicks in 46 days.** It does not have the authority to support two pages on one topic. At this size, consolidation is almost always right.
- The blog post draws **2 impressions in 46 days**. There is no traffic to protect and nothing to lose by merging.
- `/services/gst/` is 2,395 words and the guide is 1,998. Merged, with the outline in §3.2, it becomes a ~3,500-word page that genuinely covers registration *and* the return cycle — which is what the ambiguous queries want.

Keep-and-relink was rejected because it preserves the ambiguity that is causing the problem.
A canonical alone was rejected because a 301 also consolidates the (few) external and internal
links, and the blog URL has no independent reason to exist once merged.

**Steps:**
1. Merge the guide's content into `/services/gst/` under the `H2 Which GST returns apply to you` block in §3.2. Keep the guide's due-date table and QRMP explanation verbatim — they are the strongest part.
2. Add to `_redirects`, **above** the catch-all 404 line:
   ```
   /blog/gst-return-filing-guide-delhi   /services/gst/   301
   ```
   And mirror in `.htaccess`:
   ```
   RedirectMatch 301 ^/blog/gst-return-filing-guide-delhi/?$ /services/gst/
   ```
3. Repoint the **10 internal links** currently aimed at the blog post. Verified count, by file: `/services/gst/` ×2, `/blog/itr-filing-checklist/` ×2, and one each from `/services/company-registration/`, `/services/accounting-assurance/`, `/services/investment-advisory/`, `/services/income-tax/`, `/blog/12a-80g-registration-explained/`, `/blog/`. Re-verify before the merge with:
   ```
   grep -rn 'href="/blog/gst-return-filing-guide-delhi/' --include=*.html .
   ```
   Repoint all of them to `/services/gst/`. Leaving them to resolve through the 301 wastes the link equity the merge exists to consolidate.
4. Remove the URL from `sitemap.xml` and the `/blog/` index listing.
5. Update the `Blog` schema `blogPost` array in `/blog/index.html` (lines ~362–364) and drop the `<loc>` from `sitemap.xml` line 77, taking the sitemap from 15 URLs to 14 (then to 15–16 again when the locality pages ship).

**Do not ship the redirect before the content merge.** A 301 to a page that does not yet
contain the guide's content loses the only thing being consolidated.

**What actually shipped, and two things the steps above understated:**

- The redirect had to go in **`netlify.toml`**, not just `_redirects`. That file's own header says rules in it are evaluated *before* `_redirects`, so a rule added only to `_redirects` would have been shadowed. Added to all three configs (`netlify.toml`, `_redirects`, `.htaccess`).
- **The source directory had to be deleted.** On Netlify a static file that still exists wins over an unforced redirect, so leaving `blog/gst-return-filing-guide-delhi/index.html` in place would have made the 301 a no-op regardless of which config it was in. Deleted.
- The "Read next" entry on `/services/gst/` was **removed**, not repointed — repointing would have made it a self-link. Same for the "Where this connects" paragraph, which now refers to the merged section above it.
- Merged body: **1,373 → 2,460 words.** Sitemap 17 → 16 URLs. Inbound links to `/services/gst/`: 17 files.

## 7. Measurement

### 7.1 Full query export — why the last one returned 20 rows

The 20-row result is the Performance report's default table page size. The export took what
was rendered, not what exists. Fix, in order of completeness:

1. **UI export (up to 1,000 rows).** Performance → date range → **Queries** tab → scroll to the bottom of the table → **Show more** until it stops → then **Export → Google Sheets**. Exporting before expanding gives you the 20 visible rows.
2. **Export per dimension, then join.** The Queries export and the Pages export are separate tables; neither contains the other's dimension. To get query×page, apply a **Page filter** for each of the 15 URLs and export Queries under each filter. 15 exports, but it is the only way to get the pairing in the UI.
3. **Search Analytics API / Looker Studio connector (up to 50,000 rows, no pagination).** `searchanalytics.query` with `dimensions: ["query","page","device"]`, `rowLimit: 25000`. This is the only method that returns query×page×device in one pull, and it is what you should standardise on.
4. **Check the property type.** Confirm you are on the **Domain property**, not a `https://www.` URL-prefix property. A URL-prefix property silently omits any impression served on the apex or on `http://`.
5. **Set Search type = Web** explicitly, and export Image/News separately if you want them. The default "Web" filter is applied but not always inherited by scheduled exports.

**The hard limit you cannot export around.** GSC withholds **anonymised queries** — rare
queries that could identify a user are stripped from the API *and* the UI permanently. They
still count in the Total row. This is why the totals never reconcile with the sum of the query
rows. At 341 impressions across 46 days, a large share of this site's queries are rare enough
to be anonymised, so **the 75% gap is partly unrecoverable, not purely an export error.**

Reconcile explicitly: export Queries, sum the impressions column, and compare to the Total.
The difference is your anonymised volume. Record that number — if it shrinks as traffic grows,
your query visibility is improving.

## 8. ICAI compliance flags

Chartered Accountants in practice are bound by the CA Act 1949 First Schedule, Part I —
Clause (6) prohibits soliciting clients "directly or indirectly, by circular, advertisement,
personal communication or interview or by any other means", and Clause (7) prohibits
advertising professional attainments or services. ICAI's Advertisement Guidelines and the
website conditions have been revised recently; **have the CA verify the current text against
the ICAI Code of Ethics before any of the flagged items ship.** The principles below are
stable; the precise permitted wording is what moves.

| # | Item | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "free consultation", "free call" | **Removed.** Inducement — classic solicitation | Done |
| 2 | "trusted CA", "trusted Delhi CA" | **Removed.** Superlative / self-praise | Done |
| 3 | "Best CA in Delhi" style titles | Never present, and explicitly avoided in §2 | Also a Google spam-policy issue independently |
| 4 | **Review solicitation** | **Blocked.** Standard local-SEO advice is "ask every client for a Google review". Solicited reviews are testimonials, and this is the sharpest conflict between local SEO practice and ICAI | Do not run a review campaign. Reviews arriving unprompted are a different matter — do not delete them, do not solicit them. Confirm with the CA |
| 5 | `AggregateRating` / `Review` schema | **Do not add** | Two reasons: Google ignores self-serving reviews on your own domain, and ICAI treats them as testimonials |
| 6 | Client names, logos, case studies, testimonials | Not present. Keep it that way | The `/ca-in-rohini/` "who we work with" section must stay at the level of *categories* ("traders, small manufacturers"), never named clients |
| 7 | **Google Ads / paid search** | **Do not run** | Paid advertising for professional services is solicitation. This plan is organic-only by necessity, which is also why §5's authority constraint is real |
| 8 | Paid directory listings (Justdial/Sulekha premium) | **Flagged** | A free, factual NAP listing reads as a directory entry. A *paid promoted placement* reads as advertising. Take the free listings; get the CA's decision before paying for placement |
| 9 | Publishing a fee schedule or `priceRange` | **Excluded from §4.3** | Fee disclosure has historically been restricted under the website conditions. The site's existing "fee discussed before work begins" framing is the safe form — keep it |
| 10 | `/services/deed-drafting/` scope | **Flagged** | Drafting and documentation are within scope; court representation and legal opinion are not. Keep the page's framing on drafting. Do not add "legal services" language |
| 11 | `/services/investment-advisory/` scope | **Flagged, SEBI not ICAI** | Investment advice for consideration generally requires SEBI RIA registration. The new meta explicitly says "no product distribution or commission", and the site already disclaims product distribution. Confirm the RIA position with the CA — if the firm is not registered, the page must stay on *tax-efficiency* framing, not securities recommendation |
